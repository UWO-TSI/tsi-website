# Training rod

Rarity: common. Attach `art/pipeline/out/rods/collage.webp` (the reference sheet) with this prompt. Step 3 of `art/pipeline/tools.md`; the picked sheet goes to `blueprint.py` (step 4).

## Prompt

Design a NEW fishing rod for our game: the Training rod. A beginner's practice rod, like a child's toy. A sunny yellow blank, thicker than the others, ending in a round red safety ball at the tip. A fat sky-blue grip with three soft finger ridges. An oversized round red reel with a big chunky crank. Two wide blue bands. Bright primary colours, rounded everywhere, nothing sharp. Palette: sunny yellow, sky blue, tomato red, cream line. Length: a little shorter than the Flimsy rod (tier 1). The grip, the hand position near its top and the tip stay where a rod has them, so it can still be held and cast.

Match the style of the attached reference sheet of our game's five fishing rods exactly: cozy hand-made toy fishing rods for a soft low-poly island game. A slim six-sided blank on a chunky tapered grip, a round disc reel hanging in front just above the hand, a thin pale cream line from the reel to the tip, the blank tapering to about half its thickness and bowing gently backwards, away from the reel, toward the tip, two or three narrow accent bands. Every colour flat and matte like painted wood or lacquered toy plastic: no texture, no grain, no shine, no metal glints, only a soft light from above. Simple faceted shapes (6 to 9 sides), a crisp silhouette that reads from far away, no fine detail. One idea told through two or three colours and one signature part. The vibe: a favourite toy, a summer dock, handmade and friendly, a little chunky, never realistic, never techy.

Proportions as on the reference sheet: the grip about a fifth of the length and about 1.4 times as thick as the blank's butt, the blank's butt about 1/30 of the length, the reel a disc about 1/15 of the length across, centred about 1/6 of the way up and hanging in front of the blank.

Output one image: a turnaround sheet on a pure white background. One row of five views of the same rod, left to right: front, side, back, three-quarter, top. Every view orthographic with no perspective, all at exactly the same scale, the rod standing upright with the bottom of the grip on one shared horizontal baseline and the tip at the top, the views evenly spaced with clear white space between them, never touching or overlapping. Front: the reel faces the viewer. Side: seen from the side, the reel pointing left. Back: the reel hidden behind the blank. Three-quarter: from the front left, slightly above. Top: looking straight down from above the tip. The rod is exactly the same height in the front, side and back views, so the views line up as modelling blueprints. Even, flat, soft lighting from above, no cast shadows, no floor, no reflections. The rod alone: no hands, no fish, no water, no props, no text, no labels, no arrows, no dimension lines, no aura, glow or particles.
