# Applicant characters

The player and Jayden use different Quaternius characters from the same pack, with separate skeletons and animation playback.

`jayden.gltf`: `Casual_Male.gltf`, Quaternius Ultimate Animated Character Pack (November 2019), supplied by David via https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1sNi1AfenfPRrvRt5yfaj5QMMd6KKcUJ5 .

Folder License.txt confirms CC0 1.0 Universal: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . Downloaded unchanged on 2026-09-16.

## Previous unused model

`casual-hoodie.glb` is the earlier Quaternius CC0 character, retained from the recovered build but no longer rendered.

## Player

`player.gltf`: `Casual2_Male.gltf` from the same CC0 pack, downloaded 2026-09-16. Source: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1L2h3GVVJxa-_8sHnqOL-g4n7fQlD-Fdm/view . Skin, hair, face and clothing material colors adjusted for a distinct player appearance. Geometry and animations preserved.
