## Confirmed direction

Preserve the community island, established destinations, portal functions and activity purposes. Revamp appearance and gameplay in complete, reviewable milestones. Use Ponytail full mode: reuse existing code and assets, implement the smallest correct solution, and verify proportionally.

- Animal Crossing-inspired 3D surroundings with low-poly 3D characters from a unified Blender character set (decision 105 retires the 2D-sprite pivot).
- Whole-scene pixelation on by default, with an off toggle.
- Existing asset library first; propose replacements where quality falls short.
- Start with the village core of a larger island whose biomes unlock over time (decision 86); the simple default island is the first build, not the end state. Preserve saved map drafts.
- Future terrain/interior editing uses snapping and grid logic with naturally blended scenery.
- Full laptop gameplay and lightweight phone presence.
- Improve controls, feedback and weak mechanics within existing activity purposes. Broader mechanics/progression changes require explicit decisions.
- Preserve existing organizational roles, leaderboard privacy and skippable onboarding. XP now advances combat stats/builds and comes from club participation plus in-game grinding/missions (decision 11 supersedes the earlier IRL-only rule). Oracle/MBTI assigns a starting family that is initially locked. At the subclass unlock level, players may change family before committing (decision 16 supersedes 9).

## Drawn incantations

Confirmed in decision C2: display spell guidelines and let the player draw the incantation; stronger spells demand more difficult drawings. Grade similarity as a percentage. Below 50% fails, while 95%+ grants a critical or other enhanced cast. Successful intermediate scores affect spell strength; the exact scaling is still a balance proposal. The user described both chanting and an osu-like drawing interaction; microphone/voice recognition is not requested.

Proposed first prototype, not yet an approved detailed design: activating a designated ability enters a clearly signaled casting mode with a visible rune, start point and stroke direction. The player traces it with the mouse, then sees accuracy and the resulting spell effect. Use a readable accuracy score derived from path coverage, deviation and stroke order; prevent tiny partial traces or scribbling across the entire guide from earning a high score. Keep timing requirements separate from geometric accuracy unless David chooses rhythm-based casting. Score tolerances need playtesting with both mouse and laptop trackpad; this is a gameplay rating, not a mathematically objective percentage of shape likeness.

Begin with one simple rune and one harder rune in the combat foundation milestone. Verify that input transitions, cancellation, failure, ordinary success and enhanced success are understandable in the actual encounter before expanding to other spells. Propose failure costs and an accessibility assist with the full kit design. Do not promise a fair scoring curve or fun combat pacing before this prototype is played.

Decisions 52–53 settle coverage and timing: selected powerful spells/rituals require incantations; ordinary attacks and quick spells stay instant. The world runs at full speed and the caster stands still while tracing. Enemies, summons, totems and existing protective effects continue acting. Later PvP needs separate interruption, targeting and authoritative scoring rules; it is not part of the first release.

## Consolidated playable design proposal

All numbered questions through 53 are answered, but this does not establish the whole-world vision. David explicitly corrected the premature convergence: combat is only one part, and world feel, fishing and foraging need substantial design discussion. The following defaults are proposals, not additional user decisions or a settled world design.

### Combat and incantation defaults

- Weapon attack on left click, WASD movement, Space dodge and four remappable ability keys, initially 1–4. Mouse aims attacks and previews ground placement. Basic combat keeps the four equipped actions visible with resource and cooldown feedback.
- Players choose a loadout outside combat. Equipped abilities come from their current subclass kit; the any-weapon rule remains intact. Persistent minions/totems stay within capacity/active limits, and changing the source loadout reconciles those effects rather than granting extra permanent units.
- For an incantation, aim first, then activate the ability to lock its direction or ground location and show a compact rune guide that preserves visibility of incoming attacks. Tracing is a separate input mode; mouse movement draws rather than retargets. Show a world-space windup so the stationary cast is readable.
- Confirmed correction C3: dodge cancels casting. Ordinary damage not erasing the drawing and heavy stagger interrupting it remain proposals. Shields absorbing hits and summons/totems continuing to act are proposed supporting behavior.
- Initial resource proposal: starting a drawing spends 25% of its normal energy cost; successful release spends the rest. Failed/cancelled casts pay that starting cost and a short recovery, not the full successful cooldown. This lets mistakes matter without consuming all energy while learning. Tune values in the prototype.
- Initial potency proposal: below 50% produces a clear fizzle; 50–94% scales smoothly from half to normal potency; 95%+ gives an enhanced effect, starting at 1.5 times normal for damage spells. Spell-specific caps apply to shields, healing and control; do not multiply every effect indiscriminately or stack an unintended second critical roll.
- Harder runes add deliberate turns/strokes and length rather than tiny pixel-perfect lines. A visible spell-specific time limit prevents unlimited tracing while invulnerable. Test path coverage, deviation and stroke order against valid traces, partial traces, scribbles and different input sampling rates. Timing is not rhythm scoring unless explicitly added later.
- Village boundaries reject hostile attacks and reset pursuing enemies to their home area. Returning enemies recover so players cannot safely damage them from inside the village. Peaceful players retain independent access to all club tools.

### Default island and first adventure proposal

Keep the simple default island. Put HQ and Shop around a central arrival plaza; place Oracle on a nearby rise and the Wharf along the shore. A planted walking loop connects peaceful gathering spots and NPC stops. One clearly marked path leads to the wilderness/ruins. Preserve natural blended terrain and use existing assets before requesting replacements. This is a composition proposal, not a replacement for the saved custom map.

Proposed first enemy roles: a slow close-range creature, a telegraphed charging wolf, a ranged forest creature, and an aerial creature. These exercise melee, dodging, ranged pressure and Transmuter acquisitions. Actual models/species must fit the available library. The boss combines learned charge, projectile and area attacks with clear recovery windows long enough to attempt a large incantation. Avoid introducing an entirely new mandatory mechanic only at the boss.
