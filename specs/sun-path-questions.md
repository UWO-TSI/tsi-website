# sun-path (row 239, look spec §8): open questions

Each question carries the assumption the work went ahead on. None of them blocked the build.

1. **Minimum key elevation is 12°** (`MIN_SUN_ELEVATION`, `web/lib/game/lookPreset.ts`). The spec asks for "a small minimum elevation" so dawn and dusk never light edge-on. Assumption: 12°, because a flat mirror shows the sun as far below the horizon as the sun is above it, and the follow camera's frame ends 10.4° below the horizon (34.4° pitch, 48° FOV). Below about 11° the sunset glitter lane the water agent draws would sit above the top of the frame. On 2026-09-27 the clamp holds from about 18:00 until the evening phase ends (sunset + 1 h), and from the start of dawn until about 08:30. A lower value gives longer, softer dusk shadows but puts the lane off-screen.
2. **`?at` does two jobs.** `DefaultIslandWorld` already reads `?at=x,z` as the spawn point, and the look capture scripts use it for their cameras. Assumption: both forms coexist and are told apart by the colon, and `at` may repeat: `?at=0,-1&at=17:00&date=2026-06-21`. Say if you would rather the clock had its own name.
3. **`?at=HH:MM` freezes the whole island clock**, not only the sun: phase, sun, the forecast hour the weather is read from (a date outside the forecast falls back to clear) and the season blend. `?time=`, `?weather=` and `?season=` still override it. When the coordinator points the island at the shared world clock, `?at` should force that clock too.
4. **`?time=<phase>` and the options-menu Time picker show each phase at a preview time on today's date:** mid-dawn (sunrise − 30 min), 11:45 (the picked look), golden hour at sunset − 30 min, and 23:00 for night (the moon does not move). `?time=<hour>` (for example `?time=18.5`) still picks only the phase, with that phase's preview sun; use `?at=18:30` for the clock.
5. **`/lab/look` keeps its sliders on the edited slot.** Slot A (the game) is lit by the real sun (the clock, or `?at=`); slot B, the edited preset, uses its Sun sliders, and Current's sliders sit on the picked 11:45 key. So A/B at `?at=11:45&date=2026-09-27` compares the real sun against the picked key with everything else equal.
6. **The applicant island is not on the live sun.** It reads `ISLAND_LIGHTING`, which now uses each phase's preview time on 2026-09-27 (day ≈ the picked look, golden hour with the sun ahead of the camera, dawn from behind). Making it follow the clock is one line in `components/recruit/ApplicantWorld.tsx`, which is outside this branch's scope.
7. **The evening rim light stays.** From 15:00 the sun itself is ahead of the camera, so golden hour now has two lights from the far side (the sun and the 0.6 warm rim). They do not fight (both back-light the scene), so the rim is unchanged. Say if the golden-hour silhouettes read too hot.
8. **Interiors keep their room-local wall names** ("north wall" in the clubhouse and café means the back wall). Only the island and ruins comments and the minimap turned.
9. **Cadence.** The sun is one object per two-minute step (about 0.5° of travel), so the village light, the cached shadow map, the shadow box and the environment bake refresh once per step. The bake (a 64×32 PMREM) and the one shadow render cost a few milliseconds once every two minutes.
10. **Afternoons are back-lit, as picked.** From about 14:00 the sun is past the camera's side, so the fronts of the clubhouse, museum and temple face away from it and are lit by the fill only (the 4:1 look puts them at about a fifth of the lit brightness), with long shadows running toward the viewer (`specs/evidence/sun-path/sheet-2026-09-27.webp`, 15:00 and 17:00). Assumption: that is the "sun comes round in front" look you asked for, so nothing was added. If the fronts read too dark, the options are a slightly stronger sky fill when the sun is ahead, or a weak front fill from behind the camera; both are look changes, so they are yours to call.
11. **The shadow box follows the sun.** The key light's shadow box was a fixed ±24 tall; it is now the island's radius seen from the sun's elevation plus a 6-unit caster (`shadowHalfHeight`, `lookPreset.ts`): 22.4 at the picked 44° (about as shipped), 11.3 at the 12° floor (twice the texels along a low sun's long shadows), 26.5 at a June noon so the far edge stays covered. Casters, receivers and blob shadows are the shadows agent's (row 240).

## For the other agents

- **Water (waterShader, GridOcean):** `GridOcean.test.ts` asserted that the golden-hour glint is on screen-right. With the real sun it is near the middle (the sun sets 2° left of straight ahead on 2026-09-27), so this branch changed that one assertion to "near the middle". If your rewrite removes `glintDirection`, take your version of the file.
- **IslandAtmosphere (merge note):** this branch changes only the key light's shadow box there (an import, one `const`, the `shadow-camera-top/bottom` props and an `onUpdate` that refreshes the shadow projection, since three rebuilds it only when the map is created). The environment bake already re-runs when `light` changes; keep that effect keyed on the light (or on `light.environment`) so it follows the sun.
