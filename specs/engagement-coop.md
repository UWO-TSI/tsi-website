# Engagement and co-op: the approved plan

David's review of the Engagement and Co-op 1 page (2026-11-13, https://claude.ai/artifact/GDDK6ZftC4oi5Nk9LTv8Pw). Each card as it was shown, then his mark and note verbatim. Ledger rows 325 to 333 in `specs/game-world-development-plan.md` hold the decisions. The research behind the cards is in the TSI Game vault, `Concepts/Retention and spending research.md` and `Concepts/Co-op research.md`.

## What the game already has (`plan-have`)

Fishing near a friend already gives +0.1 luck and a "Fished with" stamp. A whole-shard raid on a divine fish is built but switched off. The campfire grows with up to 8 dancers and pays a daily bonus. Planned on paper but not built: co-op ruins for 4, home visits for 8, boats (barrel for 1, dinghy for 2, motorboat for 4), a weekend fishing tourney. The shop has a daily 20% special and admin-set stock, no restock timer. Volleyball is you plus a bot against bots. Shards hold up to 30 players.

**David: approved.**

## Decision: sell gems for real money? (`plan-decide-gems`)

Today gems are never bought; they come from club contributions and admin grants (design rules 14 and 76). Your ask ("what makes people spend money to buy gems") means adding real-money purchases: a payment provider, refunds, tax for a nonprofit, and parental consent for minors. Approve to start that track (cards in the Spending group); redo to keep gems earned-only for now.

**David: approved.** "they buy. gems in the shop, write that all proceeds go towards tethos as a npo and connect it to our pre exisiting stripe account"

## Decision: streaks and pressure are OK now (`plan-decide-streaks`)

The design rules say "no pressure, no streaks" (row 89), from early on; the welcome week already bent it. Your ask reverses it: timers, streaks, limited items. Approve and I'll replace that rule with a new one: pressure that rewards showing up, never punishment for missing a day (streak freezes earned in play, a login calendar that never resets).

**David: approved.**

## Decision: where the line sits (`plan-decide-line`)

The research is clear on one line. Random rewards (restocks, rerolls, eggs, mutations) run on coins you earn by playing. Gems only ever buy a specific item you can see. Never: gem-bought eggs, boxes, wheels or rerolls; paying gems to restock or skip a timer; gems to coins; fake countdowns; paying to repair a streak. Why: paid random rewards are banned in Belgium and for minors in Brazil, need published odds on Apple, Google and Roblox, cost Epic $520M at the FTC (half of a Dutch fine was fake shop timers), and are the basis of two certified Canadian class actions (BC v. EA, Quebec v. Fortnite). About a third of heavy loot-box buyers meet problem-gambling criteria, strongest at ages 16 to 18.

**David: approved.**

## The 5-minute market (your idea) (`plan-market`)

A coin shop (bait, seeds, decor, rare tackle) that restocks every 5 minutes with an honest countdown. This is exactly Grow a Garden's seed shop, the Roblox hit that peaked near 22 million players at once. Make it one stock for the whole club, so "golden bait is in!" becomes a shared moment. Tune it so about half the restocks have something notable (the strongest anticipation is near 50/50), with true rares rarer and a second path to them. Lesson from Grow a Garden: players built Discord bots to ping them on rare stock, so it brings people back more than it keeps them online; an optional ping is fine.

**David: approved.** "maybe with a 30 min market instead? is you are near by it will give you a notification. one for rod shop/bait shop and one for shop"

## A traveling merchant (`plan-merchant`)

A boat docks twice a week (say Friday and Sunday) with rare rods, shovels and decor for coins, like Stardew's cart and Animal Crossing's turnip seller. A weekly appointment and a second path to the market's rare items.

**David: approved.** "yes and it has rare and limited loot on the dock"

## Golden and rainbow variants (`plan-variants`)

Any catch or dug find can roll a golden or rainbow variant that sells for a multiple and fills its own collection slot, with its own reveal (a beat of suspense, the tier colour, a sound, an island-wide banner for the rarest). This is Fisch's and Grow a Garden's mutation hook, on coins only.

**David: redo.** "no variants for now"

## The appraiser (coin reroll) (`plan-appraiser`)

Pay coins to re-appraise a fish's size or variant, with the odds shown and a pity counter. It's the most slot-like loop on this page, so it's coins only, forever, and gems can never reach it. Mark redo if that's too close to gambling for you.

**David: redo.** "i want rods to have like variants that you can go to black smith to reroll for using coins, but its like late game stuff"

## Pity counters (`plan-pity`)

A visible counter that guarantees a rare after N digs or casts without one. It caps frustration and gives a goal to chase ("3 more digs").

**David: approved.** "yes but not for fish variant"

## Things that finish while you're away (`plan-offline`)

Crops in a garden, bait traps on the pier, fish curing at the sushi bar: they take 1 to 8 hours, so there's always a reason to come back. Grow a Garden's crops grow offline and it's a big part of its hook. Pairs with the community garden (co-op card).

**David: redo.** "im thinking how farming and bait like private stuff will work in a public lobby? like should we have our personal island"

## Rested bonus (`plan-rested`)

While you're away, a coin bonus builds up and is spent on your first catches when you return (World of Warcraft's rested XP). Coming back feels like a gift, never a penalty.

**David: approved.**

## Login calendar and streak (`plan-login`)

A monthly calendar that pays for every day you log in and never resets, plus a streak with freezes you earn in play (Duolingo's freezes cut people quitting after a missed day). Builds on the daily gift and the welcome week.

**David: approved.**

## Daily 3 and a weekly board (`plan-daily`)

Three small daily tasks (catch a river fish, dig once, serve the chef) and a weekly board that sends you to different parts of the island, with an "almost done" bar (unfinished bars pull people back: 34% vs 19% completion in the classic study). Builds on the fishing tasks and chef orders.

**David: approved.**

## Scheduled events ("admin abuse") (`plan-events`)

At a fixed weekly time, or during club meetings, an admin triggers weather that boosts luck, rare fish or dig spots for 15 minutes. Grow a Garden's Saturday admin events pulled millions online at once. A tool for officers to make club nights special.

**David: approved.**

## A free season track (`plan-season`)

A 6 to 8 week track of coins and cosmetics earned by normal play, with a new theme each season. A goal to work toward and a reason the game feels fresh.

**David: redo.** "this could potentially be good like a game pass but it might make the game feel too cash grabby, are there lighter alternatives for this, like seaonsal fish objectives that unlock special seasonal fishing rod?"

## Spending: a direct cosmetic shop (`plan-gemshop`)

Only if you approve real-money gems. Gems buy a specific outfit, rod skin, boat skin or emote you can see. Featured items rotate, but everything comes back, and "leaves in X" only shows when it's true. The dollar price sits beside the gem price.

**David: approved.**

## Spending: gem packs and a first-purchase bonus (`plan-packs`)

Pack sizes match item prices so nothing is left over, plus one bonus on the first pack. The first purchase is the biggest hurdle; after it, people buy again far more easily. Typical free-to-play games convert 2 to 5% of players a month.

**David: approved.** "i want you to have like new comer pack deal thats like $0.99 with good gear upgrade and good handful of gems thats worth liek $5 usually but its timed you know?"

## Spending: a premium season track (`plan-pass`)

A paid track of cosmetics on top of the free one, partly paid back in gems, with no gameplay boosts, and you keep everything after the season.

**David: redo.** "seaonsal game pass, again needsd thought"

## Spending: gifting (`plan-gifting`)

Buy a cosmetic for a friend from their profile. About 22% of Sky's revenue is gifts. It's also social, which this game needs.

**David: approved.** "yes we will do gem gifting, and also potentially cosmetic gifting"

## Spending: limits and refunds (`plan-limits`)

A low monthly cap by default, a confirm step on every purchase, no one-tap stored card, and a refund window. That's what the FTC required of Epic, and it keeps the club out of trouble.

**David: approved.**

## Co-op: join a friend in one click (`plan-join`)

From the friends list, plus a "looking for party" tag and a party board at the dock and the ruins. Every co-op idea depends on friends getting into the same place fast. Palia added exactly this; Stardew's "host must be online" shows what friction costs.

**David: approved.**

## Co-op: the shoal bonus (`plan-shoal`)

When friends fish near each other, every catch fills a shared meter at the spot; each notch gives everyone there faster bites, then better odds. Solo still fills it, slower. Fishing next to friends becomes visibly better. Builds on today's friend luck bonus.

**David: redo.**

## Co-op: golden tide (`plan-golden-tide`)

Land a rare spark fish and everyone at that dock gets two minutes of golden-variant chances, with your name on screen. One person's luck pays the whole group (Final Fantasy XIV's fishing does this).

**David: redo.** "no fish variant"

## Co-op: pick me up (`plan-revive`)

In the ruins, a knocked-out player becomes a little ghost for 30 seconds and any friend can revive them; the run only ends if everyone's down. Being saved by a friend is one of the strongest co-op feelings and costs little.

**David: approved.**

## Co-op: the club goal bar (`plan-goal`)

A weekly goal for the whole club (5,000 fish, 1,000 digs) as one bar in the plaza and on the club site; hitting it unlocks a cosmetic or an island change for everyone. Builds on the monument's club goal.

**David: approved.**

## Co-op: campfire nights (`plan-campfire`)

At set times a band plays; when three or more dance in sync the fire grows and everyone gets a Warm buff (better luck for a while). Adds marshmallows and a story circle. The campfire exists but gives little back.

**David: approved.**

## Co-op: duo quests and giant clams (`plan-duo`)

A few quests that need two (one holds a lantern while the other digs; one fishes, one delivers before it spoils), with a longer solo path. And rare giant clams and old roots that only open when two people work them at once; everyone gets full loot. Cheap "needs two" moments.

**David: approved.** "like well have quests that are like the coop roblox game or steam coop slop games, side quest but give good rewards"

## Co-op signature: two-person legendary fish (`plan-twinline`)

A few legendary fish are too strong for one line: a friend casts into the same splash to help hook it, then you take turns holding tension and reeling as it turns. Both names go on the museum plaque. Almost no shipped game has this, so we'd be first; worth prototyping early. Solo: a rare rod awakening lets one player do it the hard way.

**David: approved.** "lets do legendary needs like 2+ epic rod in fishing groups, or 1 legendary lod solo, and divine needs 4+ epic rod, 2 legendary, or 1 divine rod"

## Co-op signature: the club boat (`plan-boat`)

Your idea of being on a boat together. A boat with stations: helm, sonar (shows shoals and legendary shadows), a bait cooler that buffs everyone's bait, a live well that keeps rare fish fresh for the sushi bar. The captain chases shoals while the crew fishes. Sea of Thieves gives a ship more jobs than crew, so even sailing is social. Builds on the specced boats (dinghy for 2, motorboat for 4). Solo: a dinghy on autopilot.

**David: approved.** "yeah so you can create teams and parties and you will have a party boat, and you can etch your name on the boad and stuff. so you can either play solo buy your own boat, or the group pitch in to buy a bigger boat that everyone can share"

## Co-op signature: tide caves (`plan-caves`)

Your idea of fighting in a dungeon together. Short 10 to 15 minute co-op runs under the island: light combat, two-person puzzles (two pressure plates, a lantern while the other digs), a treasure room. Enemies get tougher per player (Terraria adds about 35% each) and loot is separate, so nobody fights over drops. Builds on the ruins, classes and digging.

**David: approved.**

## Co-op signature: a world boss (`plan-crab`)

A giant crab comes ashore at set times or on club night; everyone on the island can join, everyone who hits it gets full credit and a chest, and it has simple group moves (three people pull ropes to flip its shell). Strangers become helpers, not competitors.

**David: redo.** "we will do bosses later but bosses would be good, i think shore bosses should be from fishing"

## Co-op signature: boss fish (`plan-boss-fish`)

A legendary fish that fights back: one player reels while it summons creatures the others fend off; the reel only moves while the angler is protected. Links fishers and fighters in one moment. Builds on the switched-off divine raid.

**David: approved.**

## Co-op signature: island restoration board (`plan-restore`)

A board in the plaza with bundles (five kinds of fish, three treasures, two lanterns) that rebuild landmarks: a lighthouse, a bigger pier, an observatory. Progress is shared and each landmark gets a plaque of everyone who helped (Stardew's Community Center).

**David: approved.** "i think game progression that are features rather than core aspects should be unlockedwith club wide goals, like shop is a core feature, but a side feature could be the cafe or like the bank system etc"

## Co-op: the community garden (`plan-garden`)

Your farming idea. A shared garden anyone can plant, water and weed; crops grow in real time, and everyone who tended them gets a share. Rice, cucumber and seaweed feed the sushi bar. Gives friends something on quiet days and a reason to come back.

**David: redo.** "leave out for now"

## Co-op: sushi rush (`plan-rush`)

A timed rush at the sushi bar: one fishes the ordered fish, one plates at the counter, one serves before customers lose patience. Everyone gets the same tips (Palia's rule). Overcooked in a place we already have.

## Co-op: the weekly derby (`plan-derby`)

A fishing tournament at a fixed club time, solo or in teams of up to 4 (team score is your five biggest fish, with a bonus for five species). An all-week leaderboard for people who can't make the slot. Builds on the planned weekend tourney.

Your idea of your own island and house. Housewarming parties where visitors sign a guestbook and both sides get a furniture gift; a home tour board; roommates who co-own a home with shared storage and decorating; and safe permissions (visitor, friend, crew). Builds on homes (built, closed until drop 4) and the specced home visits for 8.

**David: approved.** "yes i think we should do personal islands that you can decorate your house and upgrade and invite friends over etc, it will be off to the side on a lil island connected by boat. the quest will extend to eventually youll have enough money to buy a boat to explore water"

## Co-op: mailbox and helping while away (`plan-mail`)

Send a fish, a treasure or a note to a friend's mailbox even when they're offline; water their garden or feed their aquarium, and they see "Alex watered your garden" when they log in. Fits a club whose schedules rarely line up. Note: the rules today say no item transfer (rows 46 and 95); approving this changes that.

**David: approved.**

## Co-op: trading post (`plan-trade`)

Trade fish, treasure, rod skins and furniture with a two-step confirm showing both sides (Adopt Me's scams are the lesson). Trading gives reasons to visit and talk. Also changes the no-trading rule, and makes coin values matter more (watch for alt-account farming).

**David: redo.** "trading might cause like game imbalance like op users giving new ppl op gear"

Finish volleyball's planned multiplayer phase (2v2 with friends, server-run ball), then a ladder with seasonal titles and a co-op rally challenge (four players keep a rally going to unlock beach decor).

Real-money gems only after you decide the gem question, and with the limits card. Mark the cards you want and I'll turn them into lanes.
