# shadows (row 240, look spec §9): open questions

Each question carries the assumption the work went ahead on. None of them blocked the build.

1. **Moving casters live in a second shadow map.** The spec asks for characters that cast every frame while the static world stays cached. The first version kept one map and blitted a cached depth copy back into it every frame; on the Mac mini that blit alone cost 3-4 ms (High fell from the 142 FPS display cap to about 90). The shipped version gives the key light a zero-intensity companion (`SunShadows.tsx`) with the same direction and shadow box that renders only the moving casters, and `LOOK_LIGHTS_CHUNK` takes the darker of the two. Assumption: no other scene needs a second shadow-casting directional light; if one ever does, that chunk must learn which light is the companion.
2. **High-tier contact shadows only darken ground the sun still reaches.** "Lighter on High so it never doubles up" is done twice over: the High contact is lighter (0.30 of the phase's shadow intensity against 0.42 on Light) and is masked by the sun shadow, so inside a shadow it adds nothing. Under a character at noon the contact therefore disappears into the character's own shadow. Say if you want a trace of contact inside shadows too.
3. **Contacts are registered by the objects, not listed by scenes.** Every prepared model registers its own contact from its class and its bounds, and every character registers one, so the home island, the ruins, study furniture and placed decorations now get contact shadows (before, only hand-listed village and applicant plants and Light-tier solids did). Exceptions by URL: bridges, the boat and buoys (over water) and the None class (rugs, mats, critters, background islands) get none.
4. **The ruins now have sun shadows on High.** They were blob-only by an older rule ("30 FPS on integrated graphics"). The tier rule now covers that: integrated graphics run Light, which has no shadow map. The ruins' shadow box is 36 (the canyon is 40 × 66).
5. **Swaying canopy casters render every frame.** ACNH's `mShadowShake` sways with the leaves' own wind, so it sits in the per-frame moving map even on calm days (16 small meshes on the village). The closed `mShadow` hull and the trunk stay in the cached map.
6. **The winter oak went through the extractor for the first time.** `tree-hardwood-snow.glb` predated the committed pipeline; it is `PltTreeOak4Snow` and is now listed in `extract-acnh-all.sh`. Its visible meshes and textures are unchanged and `mWinterSnow` is still dropped.
7. **Cedar, palm, bamboo and bushes have no ACNH caster.** They cast from their light-facing faces (`shadowSide = FrontSide`) and their leaves never receive. Trunks are found by name; the old export's cedar calls its trunk `PltTreeCedar4_mat1`, which the classification names explicitly. Palm and bamboo are not placed anywhere; if they are, their generically named trunks will be treated as canopy (no receive) until they are re-extracted with ACNH's names.
8. **Cliffs and terrain do not cast.** They never did, and the §9.2 table lists no terrain class. ACNH's cliffs do throw shadows; that would be a follow-up with its own cost (every cliff piece is a caster).
9. **Enemies have sun shadows on High but no contact on Light.** They never had blobs; adding them means registering each instanced enemy, left as a follow-up.
10. **The clubhouse interior is managed too.** Characters now cast wherever the scene has a shadow-casting light, and the clubhouse has one, so without the cache there the player would leave a frozen shadow where it last rendered. SunShadows manages whatever shadow-casting directional light the scene has, so the clubhouse gets the same static cache plus a moving map. No interior was relit.
11. **Held weapons cast with their character; the shirt print does not.**
12. **Two explicit overrides remain**, each with its reason at the call site: the decoration ghost under the pointer (a preview, class none) and the ruins fetch pickup (hovers, spins and rides overhead, class none).
13. **Seagulls keep their own no-shadow rule** (existing comment: silhouettes against the sky), so do fireflies and bugs by class (critters are None).
14. **The Light tier's sun-directed shadow follows the key light, the moon included**, stretched by the caster's height and bounded: it flattens below 15° of elevation and never runs longer than three times the caster's height.
15. **The before/ frames do not all land where their names say** (for example `shore.webp` is the plaza from further back; they were shot on the :3000 demo). The after/ frames use the same queries on this branch's server (:3104), so a pair in `sheet.webp` can differ in framing as well as in shadows.
16. **FPS is read at the display's cap.** The Performance panel on this Mac mini (headed Chromium, 1280×760, Metal) sits at the 142 FPS refresh cap on both tiers before and after this work; the depth-blit version dropped High to about 90, which is why it was replaced (`specs/evidence/shadows/after/fps.txt`). A GPU-bound run at 2× pixels was too noisy on the shared machine (the same build read 62 and 42 FPS) to split the options further.
