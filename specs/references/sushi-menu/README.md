# Sushi menu references (David, 2026-10-28)

David: "heres the inspo i want for the sushi menu". He supplied both images. They are study references for the look; don't copy them or ship them.

- 01-teahouse-menu.png: a folded teahouse menu (cover plus inner panels).
- 02-tea-banners.png: three tea-ceremony banners.

What to take from them:
- warm off-white washi paper with soft fibre;
- ink-black type; vertical brush-style titles beside small horizontal text;
- a red hanko seal as the one accent;
- thin hairline rules and generous empty space;
- soft ink-wash illustrations (bamboo, a pagoda, plum and cherry branches, mist) that fade into the paper;
- dishes shown on trays and plates, with small prices in a quiet column;
- a folded, multi-panel menu.
