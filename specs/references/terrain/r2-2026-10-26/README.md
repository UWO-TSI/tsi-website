## The coordinator's reading

1. **The plaza:**
   - a water fountain in the middle (`plaza-fountain`);
   - open space like `plaza-space`, and the lively feel of `plaza-vibe`;
   - flags, banners and bunting;
   - stuff everywhere so it isn't empty: fruit barrels and crates outside the shops, stalls, flower planters.

   The buildings are "too medieval, too generic", and he wants more fun ones. He says this "needs more planning", so the building redesign is a planning session with him, after launch. It's already the first post-launch item in `specs/launch/terrain-v3.md`.
2. **The path:**
   - yellowish dirt (`path.jpg`), with natural rocks along its sides;
   - not perfectly straight;
   - loose fencing with gaps, not a straight run;
   - bushes scattered round it;
   - warm lamp posts, and hand lanterns along the sides;
   - lovely at night.

   He says the reference "isnt the best": it's the feel, not a layout.
3. **The palm:** the shape of `palm-shape` (broad leaves, a cluster of coconuts, a tapering trunk) in our style, like `palm-style`, with low detail for performance.
4. **Lights:**
   - `lamp-wood-post`, `lamp-branch-post` and `lantern` are the style for the floor lamps and lanterns;
   - `lighting-game` is a side note on the overall lighting: a post-launch look pass;
   - `lamp-dungeon` is for the dungeon and cave, when those are built (post-launch).
5. **The dock and wharf:**
   - wood pillars along the sides with rope lashed on (`dock-pillars-rope`);
   - decor like `dock-decor` and `dock-fish-hut`: fishing nets, life rings, rope nets with floats, crates and barrels, lots of hand lanterns;
   - `dock-walkways` shows the boardwalks over the water.
6. **The beach:**
   - a sushi bar: an exterior for v1, which nobody enters yet;
   - coral in the shallows;
   - a gradual slope into deeper water, shallow enough to wade in;
   - surfboards stuck in the sand;
   - a lifeguard tower;
   - a soft, blended edge between sand and grass, wavy and natural, not a straight line.
7. **The forest:** seen from afar, it reads as dense, with no fine detail (the forest masses).
8. **Nature everywhere** feels like `nature-feel`: lush grass, flowers, bushes and trees, not empty.
