Signs for each kind of water (a proposal, not built)

One sign where each water starts, so you know where you are and what to fish for. The colour of the ring is the water's, everywhere it shows: the sign, the catch board's lane, the collection book.

RiverCast from the bank. Small fish all day, bigger ones at dusk. 30 kinds
Mountain pondStill water up the mountain. Opens when the mountain does. 13 kinds
SeaOff the pier and the beach. Crabs and shells on the sea floor. 44 kinds, sea floor too
Deep seaOut past the buoys, by boat. Big fish, long fights. new: from the boat

If David likes it: each sign is a small low-poly prop (one model, four plaques), placed where each water starts (by his layout), and E on a sign opens the catch board on that water's lane. Deep sea would be a new water (boat fishing past the buoys), which the game doesn't have yet.
