- subscribeArrival: the arrival store's change notice, already deferred out of the frame loop by setTimeout (lib/game/arrival/arrivalStore.ts arrivalChanged); fires on a phase or stop change only.
- setScreen: a React effect on sheet/room/island changes; it sets one variable.
- trackStep/trackFeature: a Set lookup and an array push; the network is one POST per 15 s at most, or a beacon on pagehide.
- heartbeat: one setInterval every 3 minutes, skipped while the tab is hidden.
