The Titanium, round 1: questions

Where it still differs from the references, and what to decide.

  1. What does the reserved rod become? The old Titanium (dark slate hex cups, lilac claws, lilac seams, orbiting plates) is kept as rod-reserved-slate, out of the roster, with its reference epic-titanium-ref.png. Name the rod it becomes and its rarity, and we wire it in.
  2. Machined Titanium against the metal reference. Close: the anodised run (pale steel and straw at the point, bronze, purple, blue and violet at the butt), flat ground facets with polished chamfers, a chisel point, machined rings and reel. Still different: the reference is a short broad bar with a big ground point; ours is a long rod (it fishes), so the point is narrower. Its flats contrast less in the game's stages (a soft studio room) than in the photo, where one flat is bright and the next deep blue.
  3. Awakened Titanium against the mecha reference. It reads as the reference loosely: stepped hard-edged plates with white chamfers, grey faces and dark panel walls, cyan energy in the panel lines, a ring hub with a glowing core, energy blades, a knuckle guard. Still different: it is slimmer and simpler (590 of the 600 triangles a held rod may have): fewer steps and notches, no gun body or text decals, a slim guard instead of the D-handle, and two energy blades instead of the reference's full cyan glass blade. Want a high-def copy for the close-ups (the bag, the cutscene) with the full detail (design bible, Models, rule 2)?
  4. The shop. The Titanium isn't on the shop's shelf (epic, made at the workbench), so its shop detail is the workbench's recipe. An awakened one shows in your bag (its stage and its card's icon), in your hand and in the cutscene. List it in the rod shop too?
  5. Other players. Your hand shows the awakened model; other players still see the machined one, because what you hold goes to them as the rod alone. Send the awakening too, so everyone sees the mecha form?
  6. The cutscene's glow. It glows in the epic rung's violet before and after (the library's rule: the rung's colour). Keep it, or let a mecha awakening flare in its own cyan?
