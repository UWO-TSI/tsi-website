The Titanium, round 2: questions

The old model again, touched up, in the metal of the first picture; the awakened form is the same rod opened.

  1. The touch-up. Same build as the old one: six hexagonal cups, the hex reel with three spikes, three claws. What changed: every part is polished anodised titanium (pale steel at the tip, straw and gold, then violet and blue down to the grip), with a thin film over it so a rainbow sheen slides across as it turns; each cup's rim band is dark (the panel lines), and a dark groove runs down its front and back where its plates meet. No purple glow, no sparkles, no effect at all: in a hand it is one draw. The claws are metal now too, not lilac. Keep them metal?
  2. The opening. At the awakening each cup splits into two plates that slide apart sideways, the reel's two halves part, and the claws spread a little. Between the plates a blue energy core runs the length of the rod, with a ring of hexagonal light in every cup, turning. In the cutscene the rod stays shut through the glow, cracks open a little on each pulse, and opens at the burst. In a hand the rod is small, so the core reads as a thin blue line. Want the plates to open wider, or the core brighter?
  3. Cost. Closed: 542 of 600 triangles, one material, 1 draw in a hand (round 1's bar cost 2). Awakened: the same 542 triangles plus a 288-triangle effect, 3 draws in a hand (round 1: 4). The opening is a morph target in the same file, so there's nothing extra to load.
