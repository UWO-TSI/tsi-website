## Against the band

- **Normal-run DPS** ×0.96–×1.17 of the median (band ±25%); by role: damage 1.00–1.20 (Marksman, Sniper, Gunslinger), support 0.90–1.05 (Hunter).
- **Sanctum damage taken**: the spread with today's kits is ×2.55 (at most ×3). No clear under 0.7× the median.
- **Ult cadence**: the meter fills in 61–63 s on the sanctum (60–90). The normal run ends in about 25 s, before any meter fills.
- **Ult weight**: Marksman 16%, Sniper 14%, Hunter 9%, Gunslinger 11% of the damage on the guardian (8–15%). Thousand Arrows sits at the top of it or just over (the test allows 17%): its locked barrage (20 shots a second for 6 s, each at least 1 through the guardian's armour) and the burns its arrows light in the window, which count as the ult's (abilities.ts sustainedUlt), are about 15% with no volley at all. On the sanctum the first ult fills as the last wave thins: the Marksman's and the Sniper's land on the last group (19%, 8%); the Hunter's and the Gunslinger's often find only the golem left, and the bot holds an ult for two or more (2%, 1%).
- **Mastery 20**: the family's median DPS ×1.14 of mastery 1 (at most ×1.2).
- **The guardian** (scripted fight): Marksman 2.5 min (6/8 cleared, 2 down), Sniper 2.1 min (8/8 cleared), Hunter 1.9 min (8/8 cleared), Gunslinger 2.8 min (5/8 cleared, 3 down). The bot doesn't heal there, so a run that takes much past 2.5 min can end with it down. The §3 target is 4–6 minutes; today's kits on the old bot run 2.4–10 minutes there, so the guardian's pace is left to the wave-5 pass (specs/classes/ranger-questions.md).
