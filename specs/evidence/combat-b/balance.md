# Combat balance: scripted solo runs (G2, combat polish 11)

Generated by `web/lib/game/combat/balance.test.ts` (`WRITE_BALANCE=1 npx vitest run lib/game/combat/balance.test.ts`). Every subclass at level 10 with its family stat preset, the starter weapon (of the five the gate grants) that scales with its family's stat, the kit's suggestion where one does, and the default loadout (signature, its two own abilities, the family's second). The bot closes to its weapon's range and holds (no kiting), dodges 60% of the telegraphs aimed at it (the ruins dodge: the village dash's burst with i-frames), uses an ability when it helps, and draws runes at about 80% (one in ten fizzles, one in ten empowered). Same encounter tick and ability system as the ruins (`encounter.ts`, `abilities.ts`), open ground, 20 seeds per row, 240 s limit, every roll seeded.

## Zone 1 mobs (2026-10-02, specs/classes/design-sheet.md "Mobs, zone 1")

The bot circles a crab's shell to its flank and steps out of puddles and pollen. With today's kits the band above still holds; nothing in the kits changed.
