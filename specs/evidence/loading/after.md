# Loading: after steps 2 to 4, 2026-10-27

Same method as `baseline.md` (measurement build, `NEXT_PUBLIC_GAME_WORLD=new`, headless Chromium on Metal, the trace
`specs/perf/load-trace.mjs`, 10 s on the title unless noted), built at da64170a plus main f9f4dc5d merged.

What changed:

1. **The title page loads the game** (`components/title/preloadGame.ts`): once the title has drawn, in idle time, the
   browser prefetches the island's code (its chunk list read from the build itself) and then its 345 files in the
   order the island asks for them (`lib/game/preloadAssets.ts`, generated by `load-trace.mjs MANIFEST=1`; a test fails
   on a file that is gone). `rel=prefetch`: lowest priority, off the main thread, six at a time (two on a 3G line),
   nothing on a phone or with data saver; a prefetched file is used without a revalidation for five minutes.
2. **No relinking mid-cutscene:** the rim light is always mounted (dark when the phase has none), so the golden
   crossing, the landing and every real dawn and dusk keep the same light count.
3. **Everything compiled before the screen lifts:** the warm-up compiles every material in the scene, in view or
   not (`compileAsync`), uploads its textures and waits for it (`components/game/LoadGate.tsx`; the ruins' UltWarmup
   folded in and deleted).
4. **The terrain derivation runs in a worker** (`lib/game/world/derive.ts`, `deriveWorker.ts`): the 1.5 s cell-shape
   frame and the 0.3 s shore field are off the main thread; `derive.test.ts` proves the bytes are identical.
5. **The audio device opens behind the loading screen** (`AudioManager.prepare`), not at the first campfire.

On the slow line 10 s on the title is not long enough for the prefetch to get far (the title's own 17 MB come
first); 30 s is. The canvas now comes up about 1.3 s later (2.1 s against 0.8 s: the world waits for the worker), but
the screen lifts sooner because nothing else stalls the main thread.

## The rim light and the look

Always mounted at intensity 0 outside dawn and dusk, no shadow (it never cast one), so no extra shadow map. Day, dusk
and night, before (left) and after (right), the same spot, residents walking:
`rim-day-before-after.png`, `rim-evening-before-after.png`, `rim-night-before-after.png`. Mean colour of the sky band:
identical in all three; of a grass patch: within 3 levels of 255 (the wind and the walking residents). Its frame
cost is in the perf:check at the end of the lane.
