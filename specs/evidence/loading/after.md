# Loading: after steps 2 to 4, 2026-10-27

Same method as `baseline.md` (measurement build, `NEXT_PUBLIC_GAME_WORLD=new`, headless Chromium on Metal, the trace
`specs/perf/load-trace.mjs`, 10 s on the title unless noted), built at da64170a plus main f9f4dc5d merged.

What changed:

1. **The title page loads the game** (`components/title/preloadGame.ts`): once the title has drawn, in idle time, the
   browser prefetches the island's code (its chunk list read from the build itself) and then its 345 files in the
   order the island asks for them (`lib/game/preloadAssets.ts`, generated by `load-trace.mjs MANIFEST=1`; a test fails
   on a file that is gone). `rel=prefetch`: lowest priority, off the main thread, six at a time (two on a 3G line),
   nothing on a phone or with data saver; a prefetched file is used without a revalidation for five minutes.
2. **No relinking mid-cutscene:** the rim light is always mounted (dark when the phase has none), so the golden
   crossing, the landing and every real dawn and dusk keep the same light count.
3. **Everything compiled before the screen lifts:** the warm-up compiles every material in the scene, in view or
   not (`compileAsync`), uploads its textures and waits for it (`components/game/LoadGate.tsx`; the ruins' UltWarmup
   folded in and deleted).
4. **The terrain derivation runs in a worker** (`lib/game/world/derive.ts`, `deriveWorker.ts`): the 1.5 s cell-shape
   frame and the 0.3 s shore field are off the main thread; `derive.test.ts` proves the bytes are identical.
5. **The audio device opens behind the loading screen** (`AudioManager.prepare`), not at the first campfire.

## Re-verify: the rim light without a frame cost (2026-10-27, head 14699fd2)

The always-mounted rim (step 3 as first shipped, d9a48a35) cost frames: A/B of 3 interleaved perf-bench rounds, High,
rim always on vs mounted only at dawn and dusk: village 5.5 vs 5.5 ms, v2 7.3 -> 7.6 ms (+4%). It is mounted only at
dawn and dusk again, and the warm-up compiles the island for the other light count too, so the switch costs nothing
later. Getting that to work turned up three more things, all fixed in `components/game/LoadGate.tsx` and the arrival:

1. **The warm-up compiled programs the frame never used.** Programs are keyed by output colour space; the frame draws
   the scene into PostFX's render target (linear) and `compile` with nothing bound keyed every program for the canvas
   (sRGB). Found with three's devtools hook: every program new at the crossing differed from a warmed one only in that
   field or the light count. The warm-up now binds a 1 px linear target while compiling when the drawn programs say
   linear. Programs linked while loading went from 287 to 223 (no wasted ones).
2. **The other light count** is compiled by hiding or adding the rim, compiling object by object and restoring, all in
   one task (no frame is drawn without it); toggling the live scene across async frames had made a mid-crossing scene
   gate blink and relink for 1.1 s.
3. **The golden island** (the crossing's dawn light, clear sky and dawn content) now starts aboard, unseen under the
   reflection, and the crossing's cast compiles once its loads settle (ArrivalWorld `WarmCrossing`).
