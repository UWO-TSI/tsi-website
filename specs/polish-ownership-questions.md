# polish-ownership: open questions for David

Each item has the assumption taken so the work kept moving.

1. **Which starters.** The ruling names the counts, not the items. Clothes: base tee, base shorts, hoodie and striped long-sleeve (2 more tops), long trousers and joggers (2 more bottoms), the hooded rain-cape, yellow slip-ons and sneakers. Furniture: the starter room (bed, floor lamp, bookshelf **and the closet**, since the closet is where the wardrobe opens) plus a 10-piece pack: low table, small table, study chair, wooden chest, yucca, candle, welcome mat, picture frame, log bench, tulips. One of each. Tell us if you want different pieces (for example fences come one at a time, which is why none are in the pack).
2. **Free hair colours.** Palette indices 0–5 (the dark-to-honey naturals) are free; 6–11 are the six shop dyes: Wheat blonde, Copper, Rust red, Silver, Blossom pink, Sea blue, 140 coins each. Hair styles (every bangs and back style) stay free, as ruled.
3. **Shop rows replaced.** The old outfit rows had no character part behind them, so buying one changed nothing: Sage overalls, Cream knit, Wharf raincoat, Club tee, the three old dyes, Straw hat, Round glasses and Bandana go off sale (anyone who owns one keeps it). The shop now sells each non-starter part under its catalogue name at a flat price per slot (tops 150, bottoms 140, one-pieces 180, shoes 110, accessories 80), special-pool eligible. Prices are placeholders for the admin pass.
4. **Crafted clothes have no part.** Crafting still makes the straw hat (a starter recipe), flower crown, shell necklace, crystal circlet, silk sweater, monarch cape and koi kimono. None has a character GLB, so they sit in the bag and can't be worn. Assumption: they get parts in a later art pass (or the recipes point at existing parts, e.g. straw hat → sun hat). Not changed here because those rows belong to crafting.
5. **Every homes piece is sold, starters included.** Furniture is stackable, so the shop sells all 41 pieces (the 29 new rows priced by footprint: 60 + 40 per cell, rugs 60 + 15 per cell). A starter piece can be bought again for a second copy. A home save may place only as many copies of a piece as the member owns.
6. **Finishes too.** Wallpaper and flooring are shop items, so only the starter room's finishes (plaster, parquet) are free; a bought room now starts with those instead of the striped/tatami pair the shop sells.
7. **avatar_config is server-only now.** Before this, a member could PATCH any part or dye into `profiles.avatar_config` straight through PostgREST. The guard (20260926180000) drops it from the member-editable list, and PATCH /api/profile writes the look as the service role after the ownership check. The legacy onboarding `avatar_config` field had no caller and is removed. Deploy order is unchanged: the migration goes out with this code.
8. **When the grant happens.** "First login" is the first inventory read, look save or home save (all call `economy_grant_starters`, which is a no-op after the first time). Applicants who save a look in the applicant island get the starter clothes too, which carries into the member game if they're hired.
9. **Study chime is silent until the island has sound.** The block-end chime now goes through `AudioManager.playSFX("confirm")`, so it respects volume and mute. Neither the member island nor the study companion calls `AudioManager.enable()` yet (no sound control on those pages), so the chime doesn't play there until the audio pass wires the controller in. Assumption: acceptable until Phase 2 audio.
