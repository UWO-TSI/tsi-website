# Leaf glider: questions (build agent, 2026-10-01)

None blocked; each has the assumption the build took. Tune the numbers in `/lab/move` (the Glide group, its presets and the "Glide lane" button) and paste Copy JSON back.

1. **Reach, and why the sink is 2.1 rather than 1.6.** The spec's 8 u/s and 1.6 u/s sink glide about 15 tiles after a jump off a 1.5u cliff (opened at the top of the arc), past the 10–12 the spec asks for. The build keeps 8 u/s and sinks at 2.1 u/s, with the speed you open with easing to 8 at 4/s: a walking jump off a 1.5u cliff, Space again at the top, glides **11.7 tiles** from the edge; a sprinting long jump 11.8; the gust adds about 1.4 (13.1); opening a tenth of a second late, 8.5. "Floaty" in the presets is the spec's first numbers (about 15 tiles); "Brisk" is faster and steeper.
2. **When a press opens the leaf.** A fresh Space press while falling opens it, unless the fall would land within the jump buffer (0.12 s) anyway: then it stays a buffered jump, as today. So the held Space of a jump or a bunny-hop never opens it, and pressing just before landing still jumps. One consequence: walking off a 1.5u cliff there is no moment between the coyote jump and the landing's buffered jump, so you jump (or coyote-jump) first and press again at the top. Off taller drops you can open straight from a fall. On flat ground, Space again at the top of a jump opens it for a short float. Keep?
3. **Stick released while gliding** slows you to a stop and you sink straight down (a parachute), rather than always flying forward. Say if it should keep carrying forward.
4. **One hand on the stem, not both.** The chibi's arms reach only to its jaw (shoulder at 0.47, arm 0.165, head 0.55 to 1.05 in rig units), so both hands cannot hold one stem overhead. The right hand holds the stem up beside the jaw, the stem curves out round the head to a leaf centred above it, the left arm is out for balance, legs dangle and kick slowly, and the body sways under the grip. See `evidence/glider/G-08-clip-and-leaf.webp`. If you want both hands, the leaf needs a forked stem or the hands on its rim; send a reference if you have one in mind.
5. **The leaf model** was built for this (no leaf or umbrella in the repo or the ACNH dump except a beach parasol): 264 triangles, a cupped ovate blade about 1.3 tiles long and 1 wide over the head, a raised midrib and three pairs of side veins, #86C46A / #C8E59A / #6F9A45. The first cut was 20% smaller and read as a hat from the follow camera. No reference image; send one if it should look different. The nameplate rises over the open leaf.
6. **Unlock source.** The recipe is "quest" or "bottle", like rods 4–5. No resident quest teaches recipes yet (`learnFromQuest` has no caller), so today the daily message bottle is how members learn it, and admins can teach it. Ingredients: 6 tree branches, 2 windflowers (rare, March–April and October–November), 1 red dragonfly (September–October), so it can be crafted this October. Nominal value 3,000 coins like the tier-4 tools; it is never on sale. It has no equip slot: owning it is enough.
7. **Where it works.** The village and the home island (both pass the flag). Off in the ruins (combat tuning never carries it), the café, the applicant island and the interiors. The village at this commit has no full cliffs, so its evidence glides over the 3-tile river from a jump.
8. **Into a cliff face.** Gliding into a ledge within reach grabs it and mantles up (the leaf folds). Letting go mid-air drops you, and that fall lands like any other, measured from where you let go (a roll at glide speed, a recovery from high and slow).
9. **The gust** is the air dash: one per airtime, no lift, and letting go of Space ends it with the glide.
10. **Camera.** While gliding, the focus eases down at 3/s toward the ground under you, never more than 1.5u below you so you stay in frame, and it is on the landing level when you touch down. Rigid follow and fixed yaw as before.
11. **Lab.** The glide lane is north of the lap (the map grew from 56 to 76 deep; the lap is unchanged): a one-level tower with a ramp, a 3-tile river, a 2-tile island, a 5-tile sea gap and a beach 10 tiles from the tower's edge, with a causeway back. "Owns the leaf glider" is on by default in the lab (`?glider=0` turns it off).
12. **Crafting demo** (`?crafting=demo`, dev only) now also knows the glider recipe and has its materials; its branches went from 4 to 6, so the Lighthouse rod is craftable in the demo too.
