# Privacy Policy

Approved by David 2026-11-14. Page: `/privacy` on play.tethos.ca, linked from the title screen footer. Effective December 20, 2026.

---

Tethos Island is run by Tethos Association, a Canadian nonprofit corporation based in London, Ontario ("Tethos", "we"). This policy explains what we collect when you pre-register for or play Tethos Island at play.tethos.ca, and what we do with it.

Questions or requests: info@tethos.ca.

## The short version

- We collect what we need to run the game: your email, your character, your progress and what you say in chat.
- We don't sell your data and we don't show ads.
- Other players see your island name and character. They never see your real name or email.
- You can ask us to show you, fix or delete your data at any time.
- You must be 13 or older to play.

## What we collect

**When you pre-register or make an account**
- Your email address.
- If you sign in with Google: your name, email and profile picture as Google gives them to us. We don't get your Google password.
- Your birth year, to check you are old enough to play. We keep only whether you are 13 or older and whether you are under 18.
- Whether you asked to get news by email.
- The link you came from (for example, which social post), so we know what brings people to the game.

**When you play**
- Your island name, pronouns if you choose to add them, and how your character looks.
- Your progress: quests, catches, items, coins, gems, friends and settings.
- What you write: chat messages, letters to other players, guestbook entries and reports you send us.
- What you say to island residents. Some residents answer using an AI model. See "Who we share it with".
- How you play: when you start and stop a session and which steps of the game you reach. We use this to find where the game is confusing or broken.

**When you buy gems**
- Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card number.
- We keep a record of the purchase: what you bought, the amount, the date and Stripe's reference.

**Automatically**
- Your IP address, browser and device type, and error logs. We use these to keep the game running and to stop abuse.

**If you are a Tethos club member**
- Members can link their club membership to their character and add profile details for the club directory. That information is only shown to other club members, never in the game world.

## What other players can see

Your island name, your character, where you are on the island, what you say in public chat, and your public collections and records. Your real name, email and Google profile are never shown in the game.

## How we use it

- To run the game: sign you in, save your progress and let you play with others.
- To email you. If you pre-register, we email you when the island opens, because that is what you asked for. We send other news only if you said yes to it. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
- To keep the game safe: moderate chat, act on reports and stop cheating.
- To improve the game: see where players get stuck or leave.
- To process purchases and refunds and keep the records the law requires.

We don't use your data for advertising, and we don't make decisions about you by automated profiling.

## Who we share it with

We share data only with the companies that help us run the game, and only what each one needs:

| Company | What it does for us |
|---|---|
| Supabase | Database and sign-in |
| Vercel | Hosts the website |
| Fly.io | Runs the live multiplayer server |
| Stripe | Takes payments |
| Resend | Sends our emails |
| Google | Sign-in, if you choose it |
| Anthropic | Writes replies for residents that use AI. It receives the text of that conversation and your island name, not your email or real name. |

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect players from harm.

We never sell your data.

## Where it is stored

Our providers store data in the United States and other countries. That means your data may be handled outside your own country, including outside Canada, and may be subject to the laws there.

## How long we keep it

- Account and game data: while your account exists. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete it within 30 days.
- Pre-registration emails: until the game opens and you make your character, or until you unsubscribe.
- Chat and letters: while your account exists. Reported messages may be kept longer to handle the report.
- Purchase records: as long as tax and accounting law requires, usually seven years.
- Logs: up to 90 days.

## Your choices and rights

You can ask us to:

- show you the data we hold about you;
- correct it;
- delete your account and your data;
- stop sending you email.

Email info@tethos.ca from the address on your account. We answer within 30 days.

If you live in the European Union, the United Kingdom or another place with similar laws, you also have the right to object to or limit how we use your data, to take your data with you, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Our legal reasons for using your data are: to provide the game you signed up for, your consent (for news emails), and our legitimate interest in keeping the game safe and working.

If you live in Canada and are not happy with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

## Children

You must be 13 or older to play. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If we learn that we have, we delete the account. If you think a child under 13 has made an account, email info@tethos.ca.

If you are under 18, you need a parent's or guardian's permission to play, and their permission for any purchase.

## Cookies and storage

We use one cookie to keep you signed in, and your browser's storage to remember your settings and load the game faster. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

## Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the game or by email before the change takes effect. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
