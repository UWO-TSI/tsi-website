# TSI keycap keychain (club merch)

David, 2026-10-17. A real TSI item, sold in the shop's club merch section on the mezzanine.

## The item

- **Price:** 800 gems (David, 2026-10-18; it was 500).
- **Switch:** Gateron Milky Yellow Pro. Milky white housing, yellow stem.
- **Keycap:** custom TSI. Dark navy, with the white Tethos mark (six figures in a ring) printed on top.
- **Base:** a clear acrylic block the switch sits in, with a small loop on top for the chain.
- **Hardware (silver):**
  - a lobster clasp on the base's loop;
  - a jump ring;
  - a short snake chain;
  - another jump ring;
  - a spring-gate keyring at the top.

## In game

- **Model:** a 3D model built from the photos. It has a 2D sprite fallback for when the 3D render is too heavy.
- **In the shop:** it dangles from its keyring on a hook on the merch wall, and swings.
  - Clicking it and dragging pulls it around and turns it.
  - Let go and it swings back to rest.
- **In the merch UI:**
  - The model turns slowly in the middle, with the pixel overlay.
  - Drag to turn it yourself.
- **Click:** pressing the keycap (in the shop or the merch UI) pushes it down and plays a click sound.
