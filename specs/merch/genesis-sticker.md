# GENESIS sticker (club merch)

David, 2026-10-18. A real TSI item for the shop's club merch section on the mezzanine, after the [keycap keychain](tsi-keycap-keychain.md) and the [lanyard](tsi-lanyard.md).

## The item

- **Price:** to be set by David.
- **Shape:** a rectangle (David, 2026-10-18), with slightly rounded corners.
- **Artwork:** an ASCII-art picture made of monospace characters, with "GEN_26" in a bold monospace face, bottom left, with a soft glow.
- **Two variants:**
  - **Light:** navy characters on white.
  - **Dark:** white characters on black.
- **Finish:** printed holographic, so the sticker's face throws a shifting rainbow foil sheen as it tilts.

## References

`references/merch/genesis-sticker/genesis-sticker-light.png` and `genesis-sticker-dark.png`: David's print files (2026-10-18), 1350 x 1080 each.

## In game

- **Model:** a thin sticker card printed with the artwork. It has a 2D sprite fallback.
- **Holographic sheen:** a foil effect that shifts with the viewing angle. It's cheap enough for the merch view and the shop.
- **In the shop:** on display on the merch wall, catching the light as you walk past.
- **In the merch UI:**
  - The model turns slowly in the middle, with the pixel overlay. Drag to tilt it and watch the foil shift.
  - The two variants are picked from the detail view.
