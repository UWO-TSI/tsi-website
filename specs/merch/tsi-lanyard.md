# TSI lanyard (club merch)

David, 2026-10-18. A real TSI item, the next one for the shop's club merch section on the mezzanine, after the [keycap keychain](tsi-keycap-keychain.md).

## The item

- **Price:** to be set by David.
- **Strap:** a flat navy ribbon, printed on both sides in white. David's print files are the source for the artwork; the photo is the source for the colour and the hardware.
  - **Outside:** "GENESIS" and "TETHOS" in a bold sans, alternating with the Tethos mark (six figures in a ring), along the whole strap.
  - **Inside:** the quote in a monospace face, "Technology is nothing. What's important is that you have faith in people, that they're basically good and smart, and if you give them tools, they'll do wonderful things with them." – Steve Jobs.
- **End:** the two ends are sewn together into a flat tab.
- **Hardware (silver):** a D-ring through the sewn tab, and a swivel trigger snap hook hanging from it.

## In game

The keychain's pattern applies:

- **Model:** a 3D model built from the photo. It has a 2D sprite fallback.
- **In the shop:** it hangs on the merch wall and sways a little. Click and drag to pull it about; let go and it settles back.
- **In the merch UI:** the model turns slowly in the middle, with the pixel overlay. Drag to turn it yourself, so both printed sides can be read.
