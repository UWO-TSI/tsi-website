# V1 gameplay (David's picks, 2026-10-27)

David answered the v1 gameplay plan on https://claude.ai/artifact/UAh1iey4axcvavuPXLdZe4 (collection `picks`). The plan was built from an inventory of the game on 2026-10-27:
- after chapter 1 there was no personal quest;
- the tour was 3 to 5 minutes long;
- pond fish couldn't be caught on the new map;
- the glider and dash never unlocked;
- some shop items did nothing.

**The launch date moves:** "we wont be able to launch on oct 31". The new date isn't set yet (asked).

His notes are verbatim below, each followed by my reading. "Launch" means the next launch; "soon" means the one after.

## The first ten minutes (all launch)

- **t-short** "this part takes a while and sometimes you lose site of him, so maybe a faint arrow guiding character to him if too far away, also if youre close to bram hell walk faster. make the chat like you click e once and it goes into the camera convo mode and he explains what each building does, start with hq, explain how later you register blah blah, museum is to donate cool bug and fish, go to cafe its where you can study with your friends and earn coins, and shop and say how its for in game items and REAL merch that you can buy with game currency. I was thinking HQ first and sign in with alice, then come out and bram waits for you to show the other places"
  - A faint arrow points to Bram when he's far.
  - Bram speeds up when you're close.
  - One E press enters a camera conversation mode where he explains a building: the HQ (you register there later), the museum (donate bugs and fish), the café (study with friends, earn coins), the shop (in-game items and real merch bought with game currency).
  - Order: the HQ first, where you sign in with Alice; then Bram waits outside and shows the other places.
- **t-catch** "it will pause to explain that you hold space or mouse to keep it in the bar to catch the fish, the first catch is always a fish"
  - The first reel pauses to teach the control (hold space or the mouse to keep it in the bar).
  - The first catch is always a fish.
- **t-sell** "right after he walks you down the dock walks by the ship building and rod but dont need to stop there just walk by pause points and keeps walking, no need to interact, then goes to the fishing barrel and tells you to sell"
  - Down the dock, Bram walks past the shipwright and the rod/tackle shop with short pauses and no interaction, then stops at the fishing barrel and tells you to sell.
- **t-hq** "the Alice onboard needs to be a smoother better user experience, mention a big about the lore with overlay, say tethos is a tech npo and this is how they stay connected, give a little ui tutorial, explain where the buttons are and allat. then show the qr code thing, and say that the best way to get coins, gems, and experience is being part of tsi and coming to our events, and talk about how these currencies can be traded for real tsi merch. mention mailbox and how chat works and like what all the buttons do kinda thing. have a visual ui to show what to press and what to hold"
  - A smoother HQ onboarding with Alice:
    - a lore overlay (Tethos is a tech nonprofit, and this is how its members stay connected);
    - a UI tour with a visual overlay showing what to press and hold;
    - the QR check-in;
    - the best way to earn coins, gems and XP is TSI membership and events;
    - currencies trade for real TSI merch;
    - the mailbox, the chat and what every button does.
- **t-next** "yes and make it a quest drop down top right beside mini map where you can read the quests, clicking on the quest brings out arrow"
  - A quest dropdown at the top right beside the minimap.
  - Clicking a quest shows a guide arrow to it.

## The main quest

- **m1 (launch)** "yeah first catch donate it to museum, no need to report to alice i think we need a museum npc that explains more about the museum gives 100 coins and a thank you letter"
  - First catch, then donate it at the museum.
  - A museum resident (new) explains the museum and gives 100 coins and a thank-you letter.
  - No report to Alice.
- **m2 (launch)** "yup this is good, make sure bug catching mechanic exists and fun"
  - Odile's net: catch 3 bugs.
  - The bug-catching mechanic must exist and be fun.
- **m3 (launch)** "explain how to find dig treasure, make it so that dirt will have like some type of marking and sand as well and you can dip up rare stuff."
  - Pim's shovel.
  - Teach how to find dig spots: visible markings on dirt and on sand.
  - Digging can turn up rare things.
- **m4 (launch)** "this is good, make sure theres enough rod upgrades, we should have 5 in the first launch, the starter, cedar, ... all the way to a like intermeidate rod, more rod will release later, in rod shop it will show the 3d model and show a hex radio stats bar for 5 or 6 different stats."
  - Bram's rod challenge.
  - 5 rods at launch, from the starter and Cedar up to an intermediate rod. More rods come later.
  - The rod shop shows each rod's 3D model and a hex radar chart of 5 or 6 stats.
- **m5 (launch)** "museum should have like a club collab where you need to donate 10 differen species of fish, 5 differnt bugs ... and it will open next launch"
  - The museum is a club collaboration: donate 10 different fish, 5 different bugs and so on.
  - It opens at the next launch.
- **m6 (launch)** "new more models, currently just stone and occasionally iron"
  - The pickaxe: rocks give stone and sometimes iron. New ore and rock models are needed.
- **m7 (soon, the next launch)** "this is next launch, it will give you ability to craft ships with ingredient and money, will have 3 ship models, 1 is a funny one and is a barrel and you hop in and you cant steer, you can fish out of it but you kinda just drift and its rng where you drint to, maybe you can make it to the island or maybe it sinks, users can jump out but you kinda just drown, 2 is a dingy boat, can sail up to to 2 ppl but can barely barely make it to the island, its like a wooden canoe, dangerous cuz the further you go in ocean theres more sea monsters and sharks that will attack your ship, 3 is a normal boat with like a motor faster, can outswim basic monsters but not bigger ones, and can fit 4 ppl."
  - Ships are crafted from ingredients and coins. Three models:
    - **The barrel:** one person, can't steer, drifts at random, can fish from it, may reach the island or sink.
    - **The dinghy or canoe:** 2 people, barely reaches the island, open to sea monsters and sharks further out.
    - **The motorboat:** 4 people, faster, outruns basic monsters but not big ones.

## Fishing

- **f-rods (launch)** "yeah the tiers for most items are common, uncommon, rare, epic, legendary, Divine, contraband (mods and t0,t1 only) make this tier for everythign else, fish as well"
  - One rarity ladder for items and fish: common, uncommon, rare, epic, legendary, Divine, Contraband.
  - Contraband is only for mods and T0/T1 (asked how members get it).
- **x-shop (launch)** "i realy want the hex radar graph , and you can see how individual gear and attachments increase that graph, like adding a bobber increases luck on the graph, the main rod has like add mon slots for bait and bobber, bait increases time etc and you can see how the hex graph grows and the additional is like in a different color added on to base stat of rod"
  - Rods get add-on slots (bait, bobber).
  - The hex radar shows the rod's base stats, with each add-on's boost layered on in a different colour (for example, a bobber raises luck and bait raises time).
- **f-pond (launch):** pond fishing works on the new map.
- **f-requests (launch):** daily fish requests at the fish market.
- **f-records (launch):** size records and a catch board.
- **f-sushi (launch):** sushi bar phase B (orders, dishes, buffs, the chef's quests).
- **f-tourney (soon):** a weekend tourney.
- **d-now (launch)** "I was thinking for fishing shack we will have like a board or tv or smth that shows what fishes are out rn in what area. and the seasonal one is good for more generic"
  - A board or TV in the fishing shack shows which fish are out right now and where.
  - The collection book shows the seasonal view.
